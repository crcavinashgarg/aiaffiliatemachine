This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit aiaffiliatemachine.co or register for The AI Affiliate Machine masterclass, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Internet Youth Academy ("we", "us", "our"), founded by Avinash Garg. We sell and deliver a paid live online masterclass called The AI Affiliate Machine. You can reach us at internetyouthacademy@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you register, our checkout partner TagMango collects your name, email address and phone number, along with your payment details. If you join our WhatsApp group, your WhatsApp phone number and profile name become visible to us and to other members of that group.

Information collected automatically

When you browse the site we and our analytics and advertising partners may collect your IP address, browser and device type, referring page, pages viewed, time on page and approximate location (city or region level), through cookies and similar technologies.

What we never collect

We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials. Payments are processed entirely by TagMango and its payment gateway.

3. Why we use it

  • To confirm your registration and send you the joining link, reminders and any materials.
  • To run the live session and answer your questions.
  • To provide customer support and process refunds.
  • To send you information about our other offers. You can opt out at any time.
  • To measure how our advertising performs and to show relevant ads.
  • To detect fraud and keep the site secure, and to meet legal and tax obligations.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies, including:

  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics — to understand how visitors use the site.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) — to measure ad performance and show ads to people who have visited this site.

These partners may set their own cookies and may combine this data with information they already hold about you. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage ad personalisation in your Meta ad preferences and Google ad settings. Blocking cookies will not stop you from registering or attending.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run this business:
